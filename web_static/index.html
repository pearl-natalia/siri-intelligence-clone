Before you open the app

This preview is not Apple-notarized. For your own testing, macOS offers an Open Anyway option in Privacy & Security after a blocked launch. Review the release notes before granting access.

The app stores keys in macOS Keychain. It downloads a speech model on first use and needs microphone permission. Native actions may need additional macOS permissions.

About this demo

The browser supports conversation, web search, city weather, and links to music and maps. Native app controls require the Mac app. Browser history clears when you refresh or start a new chat.

Messages go to Gemini. Voice recognition may send audio to your browser provider. Spoken replies use ElevenLabs when available, with browser speech as a fallback.
